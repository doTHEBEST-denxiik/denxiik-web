Privacy Policy for Snapzle

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Denys Borysevych ("we", "us") operates the Snapzle mobile app (the "App"). This page explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Summary

Snapzle is designed to work entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no backend server, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child. The only network activity in the App is showing advertisements.

Photos

When you take a photo or choose one from your photo library, it is used only to generate the puzzle on your device. Photos are never uploaded, transmitted, saved to any server, or shared with any third party. Photos are not permanently stored by the App beyond the current puzzle session.

To offer an optional bilingual vocabulary word after a puzzle is solved, the App may analyze your photo using Google ML Kit's on-device image labeling. This analysis happens entirely on your device — your photo is never sent anywhere for this feature, and no results are transmitted or stored outside the App.

Text-to-speech

The App can read the vocabulary words aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. This uses your operating system's own speech engine, not the App. If a voice for a language isn't already installed, your device may download it directly from your device manufacturer (e.g. Apple or Google) the first time it's needed, the same as it would for any other app that uses text-to-speech.

Advertising

The App shows ads via Google AdMob to keep it free. Ads are requested as non-personalized and child-directed:

  • We do not collect or use your advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) for ad targeting.
  • Ads are served using contextual, non-personalized targeting only.
  • We tag ad requests as child-directed under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google's Families Policy.

Google AdMob may still collect limited technical information necessary to serve and measure ads (such as approximate location derived from IP address, device type, and ad interaction events). See Google's own privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data we collect ourselves

None. The App does not have an account system, does not use analytics SDKs, and does not transmit any data to us. A small amount of information (your best star rating per difficulty, and your sound on/off preference) is stored only on your device using standard local app storage, and is never transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

This App is designed to be appropriate for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else, consistent with COPPA. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below (though, per the above, the App has no mechanism to collect or transmit such information in the first place).

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date.
