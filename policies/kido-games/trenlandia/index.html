Privacy Policy for Trenlandia

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Denys Borysevych ("we", "us") operates the Trenlandia mobile app (the "App"). This page explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Summary

Trenlandia is designed to work entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no backend server, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child. The only network activity in the App is showing advertisements.

Permissions

The App does not use the camera, the microphone, photos, contacts or location. It does not read the advertising identifier.

Text-to-speech

The App reads its instructions aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. This uses your operating system's own speech engine, not the App. If a voice for a language isn't already installed, your device may download it directly from your device manufacturer (e.g. Apple or Google) the first time it's needed, the same as it would for any other app that uses text-to-speech.

Advertising

The App shows ads via Google AdMob to keep it free: a small banner on the title screen, and now and then a full-screen ad at a natural break, never while playing. Ads are requested as non-personalized and child-directed:

  • We do not collect or use your advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) for ad targeting; the Android app does not even ask for it.
  • Ads are served using contextual, non-personalized targeting only, and are limited to content rated for general audiences.
  • We tag ad requests as child-directed under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google's Families Policy.

Google AdMob may still collect limited technical information necessary to serve and measure ads (such as approximate location derived from IP address, device type, and ad interaction events). See Google's own privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data we collect ourselves

None. The App does not have an account system, does not use analytics SDKs, and does not transmit any data to us. A small amount of information (the railway your child has built, towns served, stars, engines, trophies, the passport stamps, the Free Ride land, the puzzle reached, and the language, sound, music and voice settings) is stored only on your device using standard local app storage, and is never transmitted anywhere. It is removed when the App is deleted, and a grown-up can start a new world at any time in the App's settings (behind the parental gate).

Children's privacy

This App is designed to be appropriate for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else, consistent with COPPA. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below (though, per the above, the App has no mechanism to collect or transmit such information in the first place).

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated "Last updated" date.
